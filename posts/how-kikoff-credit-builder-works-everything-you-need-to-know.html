How Does Kikoff Credit Builder Work?

Kikoff Credit Builder simplifies the credit-building process into three easy steps:

1. Open a Kikoff Credit Account

To get started, you’ll need to open a Kikoff Credit Account. The process is straightforward and doesn’t require a hard credit check, making it accessible even if your credit score isn’t perfect. Once approved, you’ll gain access to a revolving line of credit.

2. Make Small Purchases

With your Kikoff account, you can make small purchases from Kikoff’s online store. These purchases are designed to be affordable, ensuring you can manage your payments without financial strain.

3. Pay On Time and Build Credit

Each purchase you make with Kikoff is reported to the credit bureaus. By making timely payments, you’ll establish a positive payment history, which is a key factor in your credit score. Over time, this helps boost your credit score and improve your financial health.
