IdentityIQ vs Kikoff: Which Is Better for Your Credit?

Last updated October 28, 2026. Plan prices and limits change, so confirm current terms on Kikoff's site before you sign up.

IdentityIQ and Kikoff are often compared because both show up when people search for ways to improve credit, but they solve different problems. IdentityIQ is a monitoring and identity-protection service. Kikoff is a credit-building account. Understanding that difference answers most of the question.

The Short Answer

Choose IdentityIQ if you want to see your reports and scores from all three bureaus, get alerts about changes and add identity protection. Choose Kikoff if you need to add a new positive account to a thin or damaged file. Many people reasonably use both: one to build, one to watch.

What IdentityIQ Does

IdentityIQ provides credit report and score access across the bureaus, alert notifications when something changes and identity theft protection features. It does not add a new account to your report, so by itself it will not raise your score. Its value is catching problems early: a new account you did not open, a collection that appeared or an error that needs a dispute. Plan features and prices vary by tier, so check the current plan page before purchasing.

What Kikoff Does

Kikoff provides a revolving credit account that reports your on-time payments. Plans cost $5, $20 or $35 a month with limits of $750, $2,500 and $3,500, and there is no credit check to open one. You can use the line only on Kikoff's fee and digital store, so the point is the reporting. Rent reporting is available and phone and utility reporting comes with higher tiers. Read how it works in our Kikoff explainer.

Side-by-Side

  • Main purpose: IdentityIQ monitors and protects. Kikoff builds.
  • Adds a tradeline to your report: IdentityIQ no, Kikoff yes.
  • Shows all three bureau reports: IdentityIQ yes. Kikoff provides credit report access in-app, but it is not a full identity-protection product.
  • Identity theft protection: IdentityIQ yes, Kikoff no.
  • Cost: Kikoff starts at $5 a month. IdentityIQ pricing depends on the plan.

Who Should Use Which

If you are starting with no credit file, Kikoff gives you something to monitor. If you have an established file with fraud worries, IdentityIQ is the better first spend. If you are repairing errors, monitoring helps you confirm disputes worked. If you are buying a home or car soon, monitoring plus a clean payment record matter more than adding another builder account, because a new account lowers your average age in the short term.

Using Them Together

A sensible setup is a Kikoff plan to build history and a monitoring service to track it. After a few months you can compare how the Kikoff account appears on each bureau, confirm it reports correctly and then decide whether to keep paying for both. Frequently Asked Questions

Does IdentityIQ build credit?

No. It monitors your credit and offers identity protection, but it does not add a new account to your report.

Does Kikoff monitor my credit for identity theft?

Kikoff offers credit report access, but it is not an identity-protection service. If that is your main goal, a dedicated monitoring service fits better.

Can I use IdentityIQ and Kikoff together?

Yes. Many people use Kikoff to build history and a monitoring service to confirm it reports correctly and watch for fraud.

Ready to start building credit?

Kikoff reports on-time payments to all three bureaus, with no credit check and plans from $5 a month.
